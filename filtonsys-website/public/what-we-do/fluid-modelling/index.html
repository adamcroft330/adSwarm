Fluid modelling

From one-dimensional network analysis to full computational fluid dynamics, we build models that answer the question at hand, and that stand up to certification scrutiny.

Element offers a wide range of modelling capability, from one-dimensional analysis to full computational fluid dynamics (CFD). Both compressible and incompressible flows can be assessed, with particular expertise in surge analysis and mitigation.

We choose the fidelity to fit the problem. System-level 1D models let us explore architectures, size lines and components and run large numbers of operating cases quickly. CFD then resolves the local detail where it matters: inside a valve, across a combustor, or at the free surface in a tank.

Typical analyses

  • Combustor flows and heat transfer
  • Fluid control devices
  • Two-phase flow
  • Stratification in fuel tanks
  • Electrostatic charge and voltage levels
  • Tank models to determine usable, unusable, unpumpable and undrainable fluid
