Our expertise

A demanding fluid, squarely in our core skills.

Hydrogen is one of the most promising routes to zero-emission flight, and one of the most demanding fluids to engineer with. Stored as a cryogenic liquid at around −253 °C, it raises challenges in thermal management, boil-off, two-phase flow and safety that sit squarely within our core skills.

Our team has extensive expertise with both gaseous and liquid hydrogen. Our dedicated facility at Kemble, UK features a hydrogen liquefaction plant designed and constructed in-house, with equipment for liquid hydrogen conditioning, liquefaction and storage.

Capabilities

  • Gaseous and liquid hydrogen fuel system design
  • Cryogenic fluid modelling and analysis
  • Liquid hydrogen conditioning, liquefaction and storage
  • Demonstrator and test rig design and build
  • Test programme management
  • Safety analysis and documentation

Project highlight

Safe Flight liquid hydrogen demonstrator

Under the Innovate UK-funded Safe Flight project, FSE and Fabrum supported GKN Aerospace on liquid hydrogen fuel systems for aviation. A ground-based demonstrator of a 2.4 kW liquid hydrogen system was jointly designed with GKN Aerospace and built by FSE.
